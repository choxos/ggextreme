# ggextreme 0.1.0

* First release.
* Bar chart races: `ggrace()`, `race_frame()`, `race_size()` and
  `animate_race()`, with country flags on the bars.
* Interactive causal diagrams with `ggcausal()`, including the paths an
  adjustment set leaves open.
* Network meta-analysis: network plots with `ggnma()`, league tables with
  `ggleague()`, both showing where each estimate's evidence comes from, and
  the CINeMA family `cinema_judge()`, `cinema_league()`,
  `cinema_contribution()`, `cinema_clinical()`, `cinema_incoherence()` and
  `cinema_network()`.
* Meta-analysis: forest plots with `ggmeta()` and `animate_meta()`, and
  funnel plots with `ggfunnel()`.
* Trials and survival: Kaplan-Meier plots with `ggkm()` and `animate_km()`,
  swimmer plots with `ggswimmer()` and responder thresholds with
  `ggresponder()`.
* Explorers: `ggdiagnostic()` for test cutoffs, `ggsensitivity()` for
  unmeasured confounding and `ggmultiverse()` for a multiverse of analyses.
* Nomograms of regression models with `ggnomogram()` and
  `nomogram_predict()`, and choropleth maps with `ggchoropleth()` and
  `animate_choropleth()`.
* `graph_widget()`, `graph_plot()` and `graph_save()` for every interactive
  graph.
